    Aims & Objectives

    Non-political & Service-oriented

    DUSAB works as a completely non-political, student-friendly, and service-oriented platform for the students of Brahmanpara upazila.

    Guidance & Mentorship

    Providing guidance to new students about university life and ensuring academic cooperation between seniors and juniors.

    Career & Skill Development

    Developing skills by organizing regular seminars, workshops, and career discussions for members.

    Financial & Mental Support

    Standing by members in times of need and arranging financial, mental, and moral support.

    Social Responsibility

    Fulfilling responsibilities towards the welfare of society and the state by participating in blood donation, disaster relief, and humanitarian activities.

    Brotherhood & Good Relations

    Remaining united by strengthening the bonds of brotherhood, mutual respect, and empathy among members.
